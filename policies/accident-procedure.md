# Accident and Damage Procedure

## Safety first

Your safety and the safety of others come first. Stop the vehicle as soon as it is safe, turn on the hazard lights, and check whether anyone is injured. If anyone is injured, even slightly, call 911 (or the local emergency number) immediately. Do not move an injured person unless they are in immediate danger.

## Move to safety

If the vehicle can be driven and it is safe to do so, move it out of traffic to the shoulder or a nearby parking area. If the vehicle cannot be moved, stay in a safe place away from traffic and use warning triangles if available.

## When to call the police

Call the police if:

- Another vehicle, person, or third-party property is involved.
- The total damage appears to exceed CAD 2,000.
- You suspect the other driver is impaired, uninsured, or has left the scene.
- The vehicle was stolen or vandalized.

Write down the police report number and the attending officer's name. Many protection products require a police report number for these situations.

## Document the scene

Before leaving the scene, if it is safe:

- Photograph the damage to all vehicles from several angles, including close-ups.
- Photograph the wider scene, road conditions, traffic signs, and licence plates.
- Note the date, time, and exact location.
- Collect names and contact details of any witnesses.

## Exchange details

If another party is involved, exchange names, phone numbers, driver's licence numbers, licence plates, and insurance details. Give the other party the rental company's name and the vehicle's licence plate. Do not admit fault or sign any statement accepting responsibility. Fault is determined later through the claims process.

## Report to us within 24 hours

Call our 24/7 accident line at 1-800-555-0199 as soon as possible and no later than 24 hours after the incident. Have your confirmation code, the vehicle plate, the location, and the police report number (if any) ready. Some protection products, including the Premium Protection Plan, require reporting within 24 hours to keep full coverage.

## Complete a damage report

Complete the damage report form in the mobile app, or at any branch counter, within 48 hours. Attach your photos and the police report number. An incomplete report can delay the claim assessment.

## Repairs and replacement vehicles

Do not arrange repairs yourself, even for minor damage. Repairs must be carried out by an authorized facility. If the vehicle is not safe to drive, our agent on the accident line will arrange towing and, where available, a replacement vehicle.

## What happens next

Our damage team assesses the vehicle after it is returned and contacts you with the outcome. What you pay depends on the protection product you purchased, its deductible, and whether any exclusion applies.
