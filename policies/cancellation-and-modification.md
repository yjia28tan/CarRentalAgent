# Cancellation, Modification and Late Return Policy

## Free cancellation

You can cancel a reservation free of charge up to 48 hours before the scheduled pickup time. Any amount that was authorized or prepaid is released or refunded to the original payment card, usually within 5 to 10 business days depending on your bank.

## Late cancellation and no-shows

If you cancel less than 48 hours before pickup, a late cancellation fee equal to one rental day at the reserved daily rate applies. If you do not pick up the vehicle and do not cancel, the reservation is marked as a no-show and a fee equal to one rental day is charged. The rest of any prepaid amount is refunded.

## Modifying a reservation

You can change a reservation online, in the app, or through our virtual assistant at any time until the vehicle is returned. Changes that can be requested include the pickup or return date and time, the return location, and optional protection products. Every change is repriced and you are shown the new total before you confirm. A change is only applied after you confirm it.

Changes to a reservation that has not started yet are subject to the same 48-hour free cancellation window: reducing the rental length more than 48 hours before pickup is free.

## Extending a rental

You can extend an active or upcoming rental as long as the vehicle is still available for the additional days. Extensions are priced at the current daily rate for the vehicle category at the pickup location, applied to the full new rental length, together with any protection products on the reservation and applicable taxes and fees. If the same vehicle is not available for the extra days, we may offer another vehicle of the same model at the same location. If no vehicle is available, the extension cannot be applied and the original return date remains in effect.

Please request an extension before your scheduled return time. Keeping the vehicle past the return time without an approved extension counts as a late return.

## Late returns

A grace period of 29 minutes applies after the scheduled return time. After the grace period, late returns are charged per hour or part of an hour at one quarter of the daily rate, up to the cost of one full rental day for each 24-hour period. Protection products are also charged for the extra time. Vehicles more than 24 hours late without contact may be reported as unreturned.

## Early returns

If you return the vehicle early, the rental is repriced at the actual length. Where the new price is lower, the difference is refunded. Promotional rates that require a minimum rental length may no longer apply.
