# Basic Collision Damage Waiver (CDW)

## Overview

The Basic Collision Damage Waiver (plan code BASIC_CDW) is an optional protection product that limits your financial responsibility for damage to the rental vehicle. It costs CAD 18 per rental day. When you purchase Basic CDW and follow the rental agreement, we waive our right to recover the full cost of damage to the rental vehicle from you, except for the deductible described below.

Basic CDW applies only to the rental vehicle itself. Damage to other vehicles, property, or people is handled under Third-Party Liability, which is included in every rental.

## What is covered

Basic CDW covers physical damage to the body of the rental vehicle caused by a collision with another vehicle or object, including:

- Dents, scratches, and panel damage, including bumpers, doors, fenders, hood, and trunk lid.
- Damage to lights and mirrors resulting from a collision.
- Damage caused by vandalism while the vehicle is parked, when reported to police.
- Theft of the vehicle, when the keys were not left in or near the vehicle and a police report is filed.

## Deductible

A deductible of CAD 1,500 applies per incident. You are responsible for the cost of repairs up to CAD 1,500; the waiver covers the remaining cost of covered damage. If the repair cost is less than CAD 1,500, you pay the actual repair cost. The deductible is charged to the payment card on file after the damage assessment is complete.

## Exclusions

Basic CDW does not cover the following, and you remain fully responsible for these costs:

- Tires, wheels, and rims.
- Windshield, windows, and sunroof glass.
- Undercarriage damage, including the oil pan, exhaust, and suspension.
- Interior damage, including upholstery burns, stains, and torn seats.
- Damage that occurs while the driver is under the influence of alcohol, cannabis, or drugs.
- Damage caused by a driver who is not listed on the rental agreement (unauthorized driver).
- Off-road use, including unpaved roads not maintained by a public authority.
- Damage resulting from negligence, such as driving into floodwater, misfuelling, or ignoring warning lights.
- Racing, speed tests, or use of the vehicle for towing or pushing.
- Loss-of-use and administrative fees charged while the vehicle is being repaired.

## Conditions

To keep the waiver valid you must report any accident or damage to us as soon as possible and no later than 48 hours after it happens, follow the Accident Procedure, and complete a damage report. If police attendance is required by law or by our Accident Procedure, you must provide the police report number. Failure to meet these conditions may void the waiver for that incident.

## Claims assessment

All damage is assessed by our damage team after the vehicle is returned. The final amount you owe depends on the assessment, the repair estimate, and whether any exclusion applies. Purchasing Basic CDW does not guarantee that every claim will be waived.
