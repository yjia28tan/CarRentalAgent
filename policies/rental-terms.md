# General Rental Terms

## Driver age and licence

The primary renter and every additional driver must be at least 21 years old and must hold a valid, full driver's licence held for at least one year. Licences not in English or French must be accompanied by an International Driving Permit. A valid credit card in the primary renter's name is required at pickup.

## Young driver fee

Drivers aged 21 to 24 pay a young driver fee of CAD 25 per day. Drivers under 25 cannot rent vehicles in the PREMIUM, FULLSIZE_SUV, or MINIVAN categories.

## Additional drivers

Only drivers listed on the rental agreement may drive the vehicle. Additional drivers must be registered at the counter or in the app before they drive, must present their licence, and must meet the same age and licence requirements. A fee of CAD 12 per day applies to each additional driver; a spouse or domestic partner is registered free of charge. Damage or loss caused by an unregistered driver is not covered by any protection product.

## Child seats

Provincial law requires children to travel in an appropriate child restraint. In British Columbia, children must use a rear-facing or forward-facing car seat until they are at least 4 years old and weigh 18 kg, and a booster seat until they are 9 years old or 145 cm tall. Infant seats, convertible child seats, and booster seats are available on request for CAD 13 per day each, capped at CAD 65 per seat per rental. Please request child seats at least 24 hours before pickup so we can guarantee availability. Seats are installed by the renter; our staff can show you how to use the ISOFIX anchor points.

## Fuel policy

Vehicles are provided with a full tank and must be returned full. If the vehicle is returned with less fuel, a refuelling charge applies at the posted per-litre rate plus a service fee of CAD 15. Electric vehicles must be returned with at least 70 percent charge.

## Mileage

Rentals include unlimited kilometres within Canada.

## Border crossing

Vehicles rented in Canada may be driven into the continental United States with prior notice at the time of booking or pickup. Driving to Alaska and Mexico is not permitted. Proof of authorization is printed on the rental agreement; carry it when crossing the border. One-way rentals ending in the United States are not available.

## Smoking and pets

Smoking and vaping are not permitted in any vehicle; a cleaning fee of CAD 250 applies. Pets are allowed in a carrier; extra cleaning fees may apply.

## Tolls and fines

You are responsible for tolls, parking fines, and traffic tickets incurred during the rental, plus an administration fee of CAD 30 per notice.
