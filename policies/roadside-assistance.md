# Roadside Assistance

## How to get help

Roadside assistance is available 24 hours a day, 7 days a week. Call 1-800-555-0188 and have your confirmation code, the vehicle plate, and your exact location ready. If you are in an unsafe location, call 911 first. For accidents, call the 24/7 accident line at 1-800-555-0199 instead.

## Basic roadside assistance

Basic roadside assistance is included in every rental. It covers breakdowns caused by a mechanical fault of the vehicle at no charge, including:

- Towing to the nearest authorized repair facility or branch when the vehicle cannot be driven because of a mechanical fault.
- Jump starts when the battery failed because of a vehicle fault.

Basic roadside assistance does not cover problems caused by the driver. The following services are available but are charged a service fee of CAD 95 per call-out, plus the cost of any replacement parts:

- Lockout service when the keys are locked inside the vehicle or lost.
- Flat tire changes and tire repairs.
- Jump starts when the battery drained because lights or accessories were left on.
- Fuel delivery when the vehicle runs out of fuel (fuel charged at cost).
- Towing after a driver-caused incident, such as getting stuck or misfuelling.

## Premium roadside assistance

Premium roadside assistance is included with the Premium Protection Plan and can also be purchased on its own for CAD 9 per day. It covers all of the services listed above with no service fee:

- Lockout service, including key replacement for lost keys.
- Flat tire changes and tire repairs or replacement.
- Jump starts for any reason.
- Fuel delivery of up to 10 litres per rental at no charge.
- Towing to the nearest authorized repair facility or branch for any reason, up to 100 km.
- A replacement vehicle, where available, if the rental vehicle cannot be repaired the same day.

## Exclusions

Roadside assistance does not cover situations caused by off-road use, driving under the influence, unauthorized drivers, or racing. Service may be limited in remote areas, on ferries, and on roads closed to towing vehicles. Waiting times depend on location, weather, and traffic.

## Repairs

Do not arrange repairs or towing yourself. Costs for services not arranged through our roadside line are not reimbursed.
