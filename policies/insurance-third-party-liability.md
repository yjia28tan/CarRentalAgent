# Third-Party Liability Protection

## Overview

Third-Party Liability (TPL) protection is included by law in every rental at no extra charge. It applies regardless of whether you purchase the Basic Collision Damage Waiver or the Premium Protection Plan. It protects you against claims from other people when you are legally responsible for an accident while driving the rental vehicle.

## What is covered

Third-Party Liability covers your legal liability, up to the limit below, for:

- Bodily injury to other people, including passengers in other vehicles, pedestrians, and cyclists.
- Damage to other people's property, such as other vehicles, buildings, fences, and road infrastructure.
- Legal defence costs for covered claims brought against an authorized driver.

## Coverage limit

The combined limit for bodily injury and property damage is CAD 1,000,000 per incident. Claims above this limit are your responsibility unless covered by your own personal insurance. Some provinces and countries require a different minimum; where the local legal minimum is higher, the higher minimum applies.

## What is not covered

Third-Party Liability does not cover:

- Damage to the rental vehicle itself. This is covered only by the Basic Collision Damage Waiver or the Premium Protection Plan, if purchased.
- Injury to the driver of the rental vehicle.
- Personal belongings inside the rental vehicle.
- Claims arising while the vehicle is driven by an unauthorized driver, by a driver under the influence of alcohol, cannabis, or drugs, or during racing or off-road use.
- Fines, tickets, and tolls.

## Who is protected

Protection applies to the primary renter and any additional drivers registered on the rental agreement. Drivers who are not listed on the agreement are not protected.

## Making a claim

If another party is involved in an accident, exchange contact and insurance details, do not admit fault, and follow the Accident Procedure. Report the incident to us by calling the 24/7 accident line at 1-800-555-0199. Our claims team works with the other party's insurer. Liability is determined by the claims process and, where applicable, by provincial insurance rules; we cannot confirm the outcome of a claim in advance.
