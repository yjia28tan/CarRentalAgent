# Premium Protection Plan

## Overview

The Premium Protection Plan (plan code PREMIUM_PROTECT) is our most complete optional protection product. It costs CAD 32 per rental day and reduces your financial responsibility for covered damage to the rental vehicle to zero. It replaces the Basic Collision Damage Waiver; you do not need both.

Damage to other vehicles, property, or people is handled under Third-Party Liability, which is included in every rental.

## What is covered

With the Premium Protection Plan, we waive our right to recover costs from you for:

- Collision damage to the rental vehicle, including body panels, bumpers, doors, lights, and mirrors.
- Theft of the vehicle or its parts, when the keys were not left in or near the vehicle and a police report is filed.
- Windshield, window, and sunroof glass damage, including chips and cracks.
- Tire and wheel damage, including punctures, blowouts, and rim damage.
- Loss-of-use fees for the period the vehicle is out of service for repair.
- Administrative and towing fees connected with a covered incident.

## Deductible

The deductible for collision and theft is CAD 0. You pay nothing for covered damage to the rental vehicle when the plan conditions are met.

## Premium roadside assistance

The plan includes Premium Roadside Assistance at no extra cost. This covers lockout service, flat tire changes, jump starts, fuel delivery, and towing to the nearest authorized repair facility or branch, with no service fees. See the Roadside Assistance policy for details and phone numbers.

## Exclusions

The Premium Protection Plan does not apply, and you remain fully responsible for all costs, when damage or loss results from:

- Driving under the influence of alcohol, cannabis, or drugs.
- An unauthorized driver, meaning anyone not listed on the rental agreement.
- Off-road use, including unpaved roads not maintained by a public authority.
- Racing, speed tests, or any competitive driving event.
- Intentional damage or use of the vehicle to commit a crime.
- Interior damage caused by smoking, pets, or spills (cleaning fees apply).

## Reporting requirement

You must report the accident or damage to us within 24 hours by calling the 24/7 accident line at 1-800-555-0199 and complete a damage report in the app or at the counter. If police attendance is required by law or by our Accident Procedure, you must provide the police report number. If the incident is not reported within 24 hours, the zero-deductible benefit may not apply and the incident may be assessed under standard terms.

## Claims assessment

All incidents are reviewed by our damage team. Coverage depends on the facts of the incident and on whether any exclusion applies. Purchasing the Premium Protection Plan does not guarantee that every claim will be waived.
